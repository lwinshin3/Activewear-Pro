# Activewear Pro - Brand Guidelines & Strategy

## 🎯 Brand Identity

### Mission Statement
**To empower every individual in Myanmar to achieve their fitness goals by providing premium, affordable, and stylish activewear that inspires confidence and performance.**

### Vision Statement
**To become Myanmar's most trusted and beloved activewear brand, recognized for exceptional quality, innovative design, and outstanding customer service.**

### Core Values
1. **Quality** - Premium materials and meticulous craftsmanship in every product
2. **Customer Care** - Your satisfaction is our top priority
3. **Innovation** - Constantly improving and evolving our products
4. **Integrity** - Honest, transparent, and ethical business practices
5. **Empowerment** - Inspiring confidence and achievement

## 🎨 Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage | Emotion |
|-------|-----|-------|---------|
| **Primary Charcoal** | #2c2c2c | Main text, buttons | Trust, Professionalism |
| **Luxury Slate** | #1e3a5f | Headers, backgrounds | Sophistication |
| **Accent Gold** | #d4af37 | Highlights, CTAs | Luxury, Premium |
| **Accent Rose** | #c41e3a | Alerts, Special offers | Energy, Transformation |
| **Luxury Cream** | #faf9f7 | Background | Elegance, Cleanliness |
| **Luxury Beige** | #e8e3dd | Secondary background | Warmth |

### Typography

- **Display Font**: Playfair Display (Serif)
  - Used for: Headlines, titles, brand name
  - Weight: 400, 500, 600, 700, 800
  - Character: Elegant, sophisticated, luxury

- **Body Font**: Inter (Sans-serif)
  - Used for: Body text, descriptions, UI
  - Weight: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700
  - Character: Clean, modern, readable

### Logo Usage
- Minimum size: 40px width
- Clear space: 20px on all sides
- Never distort or rotate
- Always use on contrasting background
- Monochrome version for limited color situations
